# The scoreboard

HPR Sim against OpenRocket 24.12 and RocketPy 1.13.0, axis by axis, as the project's standard asks ([ADR-209](https://github.com/nrdptel/fusionspace-eridanus/blob/main/docs/decisions/0209-the-2026-10-08-best-on-the-market-measured.md)). Generated by `cargo xtask scoreboard` from the committed reports: each number is read from the report and key its source line names, rounded as printed; a cell no report measures yet says so and links the milestone that will.

> **How far to trust it.** A cell is exactly as good as the report it comes from. Errors are each simulator's apogee less the logged one, over the logged one; the private collection's are aggregates of flights whose designs and logs are not public. A check fails when one of HPR Sim's numbers gets worse than this page, or a measured cell is no longer measured, unless an accepted decision record names that cell and the next release ([ADR-240](https://github.com/nrdptel/fusionspace-eridanus/blob/main/docs/decisions/0240-the-scoreboard.md)). The other tools' numbers are shown, not held. Speed is reported, never gated.

## Reading it

- **n/a:** a round-trip row measures HPR Sim reading and writing each other tool's file, so it has no cell of its own.
- **A cell's id** is its row's and its tool's, such as `accuracy.public.hpr`: the name a decision record uses to accept a worse number.
- **The milestones the unmeasured cells wait on:**
  - [M0.11](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m0-11): steps to a first answer, counted for each tool.
  - [M0.8a](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m0-8a): the simulator's guide, with its comparison table.
  - [M2.3c7](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m2-3c7): published flights past Mach 1.
  - [M2.6](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m2-6): an open benchmark of public flights, scored for each simulator.
  - [M2.7](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m2-7): held-out flights, predictions first.
  - [M2.8](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m2-8): how often the predicted ranges hold real flights.
  - [M2.9](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m2-9): the other tools' cells and every tool's speed.
  - [M3.6](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m3-6): reading and writing RocketPy's files.
