# The scoreboard

HPR Sim against OpenRocket 24.12 and RocketPy 1.13.0, axis by axis, by the project's standard: never worse than the best comparable tool on any axis it measures ([ADR-209](https://github.com/nrdptel/fusionspace-eridanus/blob/main/docs/decisions/0209-the-2026-10-08-best-on-the-market-measured.md), the decision record that set it). Generated by `cargo xtask scoreboard` from the committed reports: each number is read from the report and key the sources below name, rounded as printed; a cell no report measures yet says so and links the milestone that will.

**Where it stands.** 9 of the 42 cells below are measured: HPR Sim's 4 of 12, OpenRocket 24.12's 5 of 15, RocketPy 1.13.0's 0 of 15. Where HPR Sim and another tool are both measured: `accuracy.collection`, HPR Sim 12.95% and OpenRocket 24.12 12.02%: OpenRocket 24.12 ahead; `bias.collection`, HPR Sim +8.73% and OpenRocket 24.12 +7.79%: OpenRocket 24.12 ahead.

> **How far to trust it.** A cell is exactly as good as the report it comes from. Errors are each simulator's apogee less the logged one, over the logged one; the private collection's are aggregates of flights whose designs and logs are not public. A check fails when one of HPR Sim's numbers gets worse than this page, or counts fewer flights or designs, or a measured cell is no longer measured, unless an accepted decision record names that cell and the release the change ships in ([ADR-240](https://github.com/nrdptel/fusionspace-eridanus/blob/main/docs/decisions/0240-the-scoreboard.md), how the check works). The other tools' numbers are shown, not held, and so is speed.

## Reading it

- **A cell's id** is its row's and its tool's, such as `accuracy.public.hpr`: the name a decision record uses to accept a worse number.
- **Not measured** means no committed report measures the cell yet; its link names the milestone that will. Those milestones, and the cells each fills:
  - [M0.11, steps to a first answer](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m0-11): `steps.first-answer.hpr`, `steps.first-answer.openrocket`, `steps.first-answer.rocketpy`.
  - [M0.8a, the simulator's guide](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m0-8a): `features.count.hpr`, `features.count.openrocket`, `features.count.rocketpy`.
  - [M2.3c7, published flights past Mach 1](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m2-3c7): `accuracy.supersonic.hpr`.
  - [M2.6, an open benchmark](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m2-6): `accuracy.public.openrocket`, `bias.public.openrocket`.
  - [M2.7, held-out flights](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m2-7): `accuracy.held-out.hpr`, `accuracy.held-out.openrocket`, `bias.held-out.hpr`, `bias.held-out.openrocket`.
  - [M2.8, honest uncertainty](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m2-8): `uncertainty.range-50.hpr`, `uncertainty.range-90.hpr`.
  - [M2.9, the other tools' cells](https://hpr.fusionspace.co/decisions-and-roadmap.html#m2-9): `accuracy.public.rocketpy`, `accuracy.collection.rocketpy`, `accuracy.held-out.rocketpy`, `accuracy.supersonic.openrocket`, `accuracy.supersonic.rocketpy`, `bias.public.rocketpy`, `bias.collection.rocketpy`, `bias.held-out.rocketpy`, `uncertainty.range-50.openrocket`, `uncertainty.range-50.rocketpy`, `uncertainty.range-90.openrocket`, `uncertainty.range-90.rocketpy`, `speed.flight.hpr`, `speed.flight.openrocket`, `speed.flight.rocketpy`, `interop.changed.rocketpy`, `interop.failed.rocketpy`, `interop.largest.rocketpy`.
- **n/a:** a round-trip row measures HPR Sim reading and writing another tool's file, so HPR Sim has no cell of its own there.

## Accuracy

How far each simulator's apogee lands from the logged one, its sign dropped, averaged over the flights: the mean absolute error, per cent of the logged apogee. The public flights are RocketPy's example rockets whose altimeter logs are published; the private collection's are logged flights whose owners allow only aggregates. Held-out flights are ones no model or setting was tuned on, predicted before their logs are compared.
