# Awareness calendar: heritage, health, access, space and safety days by month

**Bottom line:** a twelve-month table of observances the docs site could mark with one thin
banner and one footer line, each with its date rule, region, source and a draft line of at most
90 characters. It is evidence for a later milestone; how to choose and word the lines is in
[awareness practice](awareness-practice.md). Every date was read on 2026-10-14. Some organizer
sites refused automated reads, so their rows rest on a secondary page or are unverified, and say
so. Check the year-specific rows and every U row again before they ship.

**Marks** after each date: **P** read on the organizer's or a government page; **S** only on a
secondary page (the organizer's was blocked, missing or silent); **C** calendar arithmetic from a
rule a P or S page gives; **U** not verified (carried from Motor Finder or general knowledge);
"n/a" a season, which makes no dated claim. **G**, the group: **a** heritage and identity, **b**
health and mental health, **c** accessibility and disability, **d** STEM and space, **e**
rocketry and launch safety, **f** environment; "solemn" marks a day that takes the banner alone.
**Region:** INTL (UN or worldwide), US, CA, UK, IE, AU. The source is the page read; an arrow after
a banner line names its link when that differs. Where the research recorded only a site and a page
name, the link goes to the site. Rows cite the organizer or the Library of Congress (LoC)
consortium sites, not US proclamations, which change with each administration.
