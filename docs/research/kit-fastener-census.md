# Which shear pins, rivets and rail buttons HPR dual-deploy kits carry

[Hobby airframe tolerances](hobby-airframe-tolerances.md) §4–§6 sized each head on a hobby airframe but left their count
to [excrescence drag](excrescence-drag.md) §4's invented 6–8. This note counts them in 33 public kit manuals from five
makers, dual-deploy or (3 of them) dual-deploy with a bay sold apart. **In short: a kit's manual puts a median of 3
proud heads on the outside (0–6), not 6–8, and about two in three of them are push-rivet domes at the fixed av-bay
joint, not shear pins.** No manual requires pins at the drogue joint. Pins, where a manual counts them, are 1–3 (mostly
three) 2-56 nylon screws at the nose; only Giant Leap says to trim them. Rail buttons are screwed on in 23 of 33 kits,
but only Apogee names the head (a 6-32 flat head in a countersunk button); the other 19 never state the head type, so
the rail-button head row stays "0 or 0.4%". Redone with the census count (Inference), the heads row falls from 0.5% to
about 0.2% of C_D at 98 mm, and the clean airframe's share of the +8.73% apogee bias from 3–8% to about 2–6%. How far to
trust it: the counts are what each manual says, read page by page; what builders actually fit (extra pins, untrimmed
heads, a different button) is in no manual and is not counted.

## 1. What was read

- **33 dual-deploy or dual-deploy-capable kit manuals:** Giant Leap 5, LOC Precision 3, Madcow 14, Wildman 6, Apogee
  5 [A2]–[A22], [B1]–[B21]. Madcow's four 4 in fiberglass kits share one text that differs only in name, fin and
  nose-tip lines [A17], so they are counted as four kits with the same hardware.
- **Rail hardware only:** 16 single-deploy sheets (Giant Leap 4, LOC 10, Madcow 2) [A7], [A8], [A15], [A21], [A23].
- **Listings only:** Binder Design's archived pages [B22]–[B24] (§4); no Binder kit manual was reached.
- **Joints.** Nose: cone to main tube (head-end kits: to the av-bay). Bay: av-bay to the tube it stays fixed to.
  Drogue: booster to av-bay. "Proud heads" are outside pins and rivets; rail hardware is counted apart.

- **Giant Leap's pin counts are inferred.** Its three smaller manuals mark the nose joint every 2.45 in [A2] p. 16,
  [A3] p. 21, [A4] p. 24; each circumference over 2.45 in gives about 3, 4 and 5. The same sentence spans three
  diameters, so it may be boilerplate. Those pins are then trimmed, so they are counted as 0 proud.
- **Madcow's 2.6 in counts disagree:** the bay sheet drills 4 rivets [A19], the hardware pack lists 2 [A22].
- **Wildman's generic sheet** sets pins in 5/64 in holes and three rivets in 5/32 in holes [B1] pp. 4, 6, and
  self-taps a 1010 button's 8-32 screw into a 1/8 in hole [B3] p. 11; no Wildman sheet states a head type.
- **Apogee's 4 in kits** list 2 × 13061 buttons, 2 × 13065 6-32 × 3/8 in flat Phillips screws, 2 × 13066 weld nuts and

  friction fit). The Mako's screw carries the same part number, 13065 [B17] p. 2.
- **Excluded as internal:** bay screws inside the tube [A5] p. 4, [A6] pp. 43–44, [B16] p. 6, motor retainers, and
  button-screw tips, which manuals say to epoxy over, cut short or grind flush [A20] p. 2, [B7] p. 18, [B11] p. 12.

## 3. Counts across the 33 manuals

- **Proud heads per kit (pins and rivets):** countable in 29 of 33 (Talon 6 and the three LOC kits leave a count
  open). They run 0–6: seven kits at 0, three at 2, nine at 3, three at 4 and seven at 6; median 3, mean 3.0 (87
  heads over 29 kits). Madcow's 14 have median 3.5, Wildman's 6 mean 4.2, Apogee's 5 mean 2.4, Giant Leap 0 once
  trimmed (3–5 if not).
- **Rivets outnumber pins two to one:** 57 of the 87 heads are push rivets, 30 are pins (counted from the table).
  The rivet is a dome: Madcow's medium rivet head is 0.315 in (8.0 mm) across and 0.071 in (1.80 mm) tall [A22];
  the tolerances note gives Giant Leap's as 8.05 × 1.98 mm and LOC's as 8 × 1.8 mm.
- **Nose pins:** counted in 11 of 33. Madcow (5 kits) always gives three nylon screws in 5/64 in holes [A17], [A18],
  printed "2-256" (read as 2-56); Wildman 1–3, three in 4 of 6 [B4]–[B9], 2-56 where stated [B1], [B6], [B7]. Giant Leap
  implies 3–5, and two manuals advise pins with no count. The other 17 give the nose no counted pin (none, friction fit,
  tape, or not fastened), including all five Apogee kits. Only Dark Star Jr ships pins, uncounted [B4] p. 1, [B21].
- **Bay joint:** 2–4 rivets where it comes apart, 3 the most common (Madcow 2 at 1.6–2.2 in, 3 at 3–4 in, 4 at
  2.6 in [A17]–[A19]; Wildman 2–3; Apogee 3; LOC HyperLOC 3 screws or rivets [A9] p. 2). Giant Leap and the Super DX3
  epoxy it [A3] p. 14, [A21].
- **Drogue joint:** no manual specifies a fastener; three Giant Leap manuals call pins there optional [A2]–[A4].
- **Trimmed pins:** 3 of the 16 manuals that use or advise pins say to trim them, all Giant Leap, with end-cutting
  pliers [A2] p. 16, [A3] p. 21, [A4] p. 24. Apogee sells its 2-56 × 1/4 in pins as round slotted and its 4-40 ×
  5/16 in as fillister heads [B19], so a pin left as sold stands proud (Inference).

## 4. Rail hardware

| what ships | kits of 33 | which | screw head |
|---|---|---|---|
| one-piece button, screwed straight on | 17 | LOC 3, Madcow 14 | not stated (Madcow #6 wood, LOC #8) |
| one-piece countersunk button | 4 | Apogee (Mako by part number) | 6-32 flat, flush [B13]–[B15] |
| button with a nylon spacer | 2 | Giant Leap Talon 6, Sledgehammer | not stated |
| aluminum guide, glued | 3 | Giant Leap | no screw |
| launch lug, glued | 1 | Apogee Katana Jr | no screw |
| none; the builder fits a 1010 button | 6 | Wildman | 8-32, not stated |

- **Only 4 of 33 kits are known to fly a flush screw, and none states a proud one.** The 19 screwed buttons with an
  unstated head (17 one-piece and 2 with a spacer), and Wildman's 6 builder-fitted ones, could be either. Countersunk
  guides on Madcow's site are a separately sold Aeropack part, not the kit's [A22].
- **Two kits add a nylon spacer** (Giant Leap's 6 and 7.5 in), the nearest to the three-piece kit (button, spacer,
  washers) whose pan-style head the tolerances note put 2.2–2.4 mm proud (§5); their head isn't stated. The
  single-deploy sheets show the same mix: 4 Giant Leap glue guides, 2 Madcow and 4 LOC screw buttons on, 6 LOC use a lug
  [A7], [A8], [A15], [A21], [A23]. All 13 of Binder's 4 in kits list rail buttons (about 6 say Delrin) [B22]; its button
  page sells one-piece Delrin [B23].
- **General guidance:** Apogee's pin page says to drill three holes per joint and quotes 64.24 lb to shear three
  2-56 pins and 119.2 lb for three 4-40 [B19]; its rivet page says to use at least one more rivet than pins at the
  separating end [B20]. No NAR or TRA document giving a per-joint count was found.

## 5. The heads rows redone (Inference)

[Hobby airframe tolerances](hobby-airframe-tolerances.md) §6 kept §4's 6–8 heads, at 0.5% of C_D typical (0.2–1.5%)
at 98 mm and 0.8% (0.35–1.8%) at 54 mm. Scaling the typical by count (3 of 7) and the top by the census's most
(6 of 8), with each head's size and coefficient unchanged:

| row | 98 mm: % of C_D, typical (range) | 54 mm: % of C_D, typical (range) |
|---|---|---|
| heads, tolerances note §6 | 0.5 (0.2–1.5) | 0.8 (0.35–1.8) |
| heads, this census | 0.21 (0–1.1) | 0.34 (0–1.35) |
| 2 rail-button screw heads | 0 or 0.4 (0–0.7), unchanged | 0 or 1.3 (0–2.3), unchanged |
| **clean airframe, total** | **0.48–0.88 (0.08–3.5)** | **0.88–2.18 (0.17–7.0)** |

- **Against the bias:** at −0.31% to −0.57% of apogee per 1% of drag ([apogee bias](apogee-bias.md)), 0.48–0.88% of
  C_D at 98 mm is −0.15% to −0.50% of apogee, 2–6% of the +8.73% bias (the tolerances note: 3–8%). At 54 mm it is
  −0.27% to −1.2%, 3–14% (from 5–17%).
- **The scaling keeps a mix the census changes:** most heads are 8 mm rivet domes rather than 4 mm pin heads, so
  per-head drag at the census's mix is likely above the tolerances note's typical; this note doesn't redo it.

## What the project can use

- **A default head count** for any optional heads term: 3 rivet domes 8 mm across and 1.8 mm proud at the av-bay
  joint, plus 0 or 3 pin heads (2-56 nylon, 1.1–1.35 mm) at the nose, untrimmed unless the design says so; 0 heads at
  the drogue joint. Range 0–6.
- **For [#870](https://github.com/nrdptel/fusionspace-eridanus/issues/870):** a screw height of 0 is the documented
  case for Apogee's countersunk button; a proud head is undocumented in every kit read, so the 2 mm test case is the
  three-piece or builder-fitted pan-head case, not a typical kit's.

## What this suggests

- **Lower the heads row** in the excrescence share of the +8.73% bias to about 0.2% of C_D at 98 mm (0–1.1%), and
  the clean-airframe share to about 2–6% of the bias.
- **Settle the rail row with a photo:** a picture or drawing of Madcow's buttons on their #6 wood screws and LOC's
  1000-series guides would show whether the head is flush (Inference: those two cover 17 of the 33 kits).

## Open questions

- **Builder practice:** how often builders add pins where a manual is silent, leave pin heads on, or swap buttons.
  Build threads on forums and review sites were not surveyed; a count would need many of them.
- **Rail-button heads** for Madcow's, LOC's and Binder's buttons, and the head on Giant Leap's spacer buttons.
- **Kits not reached:** Binder kit manuals; LOC's Bruiser EXP, Force Ten and Magnum (no PDF on their pages [A16]);
  Performance Rocketry (no instructions ship, by reviews); Eggtimer's EZ-DD.
- **Per-head drag of an 8 mm push-rivet dome** at a hobby rocket's Reynolds number, unmeasured.
